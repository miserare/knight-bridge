# Privacy policy — Knight Bridge 0.1.1

Effective September 28, 2026.

Knight Bridge runs locally. It has no developer-operated server and collects no analytics, advertising identifiers, diagnostic uploads, passwords, personal access tokens, or cookies. It does not read browsing history or synchronize storage.

When you choose Analyze on Lichess, the extension validates the selected/exported PGN or FEN and transfers it to **lichess.org** using your normal browser session. PGN may contain player names, ratings, dates, time control, comments, and source links. Normal Lichess game imports are publicly accessible. Positions travel in a Lichess analysis URL and may therefore appear in browser history. This is disclosed before the first transfer. Do not transfer private material you do not want Lichess to receive.

The extension stores up to 20 transfer jobs locally: normalized game content, a content fingerprint, source/destination tab IDs, job ID, times, status, and the resulting Lichess URL when observed. Jobs expire 30 minutes after creation. If you explicitly choose **Load PGN or FEN from this Chess.com tab**, that selected export is also kept locally for up to 30 minutes so it can fill the popup if Chess.com's Share dialog closes it. A new active live game discards the prior draft. Expired content is no longer served; physical deletion occurs on the next extension action or its one-minute cleanup alarm while the browser is running. If the browser is closed/asleep, cleanup resumes when it next runs. The privacy-notice choice remains until cleared or the extension is removed.

Use the popup's **Clear local extension data** action to delete these local records immediately and reset the notice. This does not delete public games already imported on Lichess. Browser history, website storage, and Lichess retention are controlled by those products. Visit [Lichess privacy](https://lichess.org/privacy) for its policy.

Only the required Chess.com and Lichess origins are accessed. No game content is logged to the console. Private browsing is disabled. The extension is independent and is not endorsed by Chess.com or Lichess.

Before public submission, the distributor must publish this policy at a stable HTTPS URL and supply their support contact in the store listing. No contact identity has been fabricated in this draft.
